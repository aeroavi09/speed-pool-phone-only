Speed Pool

This phone is the camera, the shot clock and the speaker. Tape it to the ceiling over the middle of the table with its long side along the table.

Front camera: tape it screen-down so players can read the clock. Back ultra-wide: sees more of the table, but the screen faces the ceiling, so you only hear the clock.

Ball movement

With nothing moving the bar should sit at or near zero. Roll a ball and it jumps. Past the red line counts as a shot; the clock starts once it stays left of the green line for the Stop time.

Game

Start delay
  1. Play normal pool. You keep track of whose shot it is.
  2. The clock starts as soon as every ball has stopped.
  3. It stops the moment any ball starts rolling.
  4. After a scratch, it starts when the balls stop or someone reaches into the pocket for the cue ball.
  5. At 0 the buzzer sounds, then the clock starts again for the next shot.

The delay gives you time to climb down. On iPhone, turn the ringer switch on or the clock is silent.
